The system

How an artist gets built.

Five stages, one repeatable system — from the first sentence of a character bio to a living artist property.

  1. Concept

    Who they are, what market they belong to, and why anyone would care.

  2. Backstory

    Origin, references, the people around them. Written like a bio, not a brief.

  3. Visual & voice identity

    A locked face, a wardrobe language, a tone of speech and a singing voice.

  4. First body of work

    A coherent set — stills, at least one video, a track if the artist sings.

  5. Launch

    Social presence, press-ready assets, and a home page that outlives any platform.

How the system works

Built to be measured,
not simply launched.

Every artist under Vanity Projects gets a full review of streaming and social numbers every week — an actual audit against the benchmarks that artist has committed to. Dips in engagement or stalled growth are caught inside a week, while wins are flagged in real time.

Every rollout moves through 30, 45, 60 and 90-day checkpoints. The same framework drove the evolution of Slim's branding into the Slim Experience and is now used across artist rollouts.

Applied / Layla Grant “BAD GIRL” campaign

Paid ads live day one, a duet/remix challenge, creator seeding staged in two waves and full roster cross-promotion — each phase checked against the same benchmarked checkpoints.

Tour semantics

A tour that behaves
like a format.

Each stop runs a repeatable content arc — local music scene, local voices, show-day finale — so the story keeps moving city to city instead of resetting.

The rollout structure has been documented as licensable IP, making the format a product, not just a tour.
