Lagos Delivers: The Slim Experience World Tour Lights Up Nigeria, Sets Its Sights On Manila

A night of Afrobeats, Afro R&B, hip-hop, and alt-pop united the world's first AI-generated concert tour — Manila tickets on sale now for October 4.

The Slim Experience World Tour 2026 touched down in Lagos, and the city showed up and showed out. From the first note to the last, the night was a full-blown celebration of sound, culture, and the kind of energy that only a room full of real fans can bring.

The lineup brought the heat across the board. DJ Paola, Ivyworld, SAINE, NIATEAZE, PRINCESS, NOVA REIGN, KORIN OLUWA, INDHYRAH, and LAYLAGRANT each took their turn and delivered, every single set landing clean, start to finish. The blend of styles, Afrobeats into Afro R&B into hip-hop into alt-pop, moderated seamlessly by an international DJ, made for one of the most dynamic nights this tour has put on yet.

None of it happens without the team behind the scenes. A big thank-you to our premier ticketing partner, Promotix, for their support in bringing the night to life, and to DistroKid for powering the hits that carried the show from open to close.

Now the tour turns its attention to Manila. The Slim Experience touches down in the Philippines on October 4, and tickets are on sale now. The Philippines has one of the most passionate, plugged-in fan communities anywhere in the world, and we're bringing a show built to match that energy.

Get your tickets now, Manila. This one's for you.

About The Slim Experience World Tour

The Slim Experience World Tour 2026 is a 12-stop global rollout and the first solely virtual, AI-generated concert tour, built off real-time DistroKid analytics and bringing next-generation AI artistry to cities worldwide.

Fresh Off Lagos, The Slim Experience World Tour Sets Its Sights On Manila

The world's first solely virtual, AI-generated concert tour brings its sound, its energy, and its movement to the Philippines.

The Slim Experience World Tour 2026 just wrapped an unforgettable stop in Lagos, and the momentum isn't slowing down. Next stop: Manila, bringing the full spectrum of culture, sound, and vibes to the city and the people of the Philippines.

The Slim Experience is the first tour of its kind: a fully virtual, AI-generated concert experience built exclusively off real-time analytics from DistroKid, the digital service provider recently acquired by CVC Capital Partners in a deal reportedly valued near $2 billion. Powered by the hits of DistroKid and presented with the support of premier ticketing partner Promotix, the tour turns raw streaming data into a live, immersive experience unlike anything else on the road today.

Lagos showed what happens when that data-driven artistry meets real, on-the-ground energy, and the response was electric. It confirmed what The Slim Experience has been building toward from day one: this is a global cultural moment, and every stop adds its own flavor to the story.

Manila is next, and the anticipation is already building. The Philippines has one of the most passionate, plugged-in fan communities in the world, and the team is bringing a show designed to match that energy, filtered through the culture and spirit of Manila itself.

More details on the Manila stop, including the full artist lineup and event specifics, will be announced soon. Stay close.
